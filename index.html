01 · About

Understanding people comes first.

What I enjoy most is helping someone put into words what they're looking for, even when they're not quite sure themselves.

I learned it first as a financial advisor at NTUC Income. Across more than 100 clients, the job was about trust more than products. I preferred to be upfront about who I was, then take the time to understand what each person actually needed.

I came to Zeppelin & Co. as a customer, floored by the service and the excitement of new gear arriving every other week. Now I'm part of it. I help people put words to how sound makes them feel, and match them to something that fits, from their first pair of earphones to their first top-of-the-line piece. I also shape how people meet Zeppelin as a brand, through photography, design and content.

A pair of headphones can tick every box on paper and still not feel right. So I listen first. It's the same approach I bring to every shoot and every design, and it's why I'm studying Psychology at SUSS.

Bryce & Kimmel · Launch · 7–17 July 2026

Two flowers, one debut.

Photography · Illustration cards · Backdrop concept

  1. The person

    A Singapore founder launching his first luxury IEMs. Before shooting, I learned what inspired him and how he wanted people to feel.

  2. The idea

    Each product given its own botanical setting: tile backdrops and hand-drawn illustration cards, Osmanthus for one, Lily of the Valley for the other.

  3. The work

    Tile flat-lays for character, clean studio shots for detail. Nobody sees the hours of light, reshoots and cable-taming behind a frame, and that's the point.

StereoNet Singapore · Booth H12-22 · 10–12 July 2026

21 brands. One voice.

Poster design · Event branding · Daily photo & video coverage

  1. The people

    Zeppelin & Co.'s first StereoNet show, with 21 partner brands, each with its own logo, voice and fans, now brought under one banner.

  2. The idea

    Used paper textures, burnt-orange blocks, cut-out product images and a vertical Zeppelin wordmark to connect the designs. Every brand keeps its identity; the booth reads as one space.

  3. The work

    Designed 14 posters. Photographed and filmed the event across 3 days. Focused on people discovering something new: listening, asking questions and sharing their reactions.
